How the system works

1

Apply through eCitizen Simulation

Applicants begin the process by accessing the simulated eCitizen Civil Registration Services portal and submitting a birth certificate application.

2

Book through Huduma Kenya Simulation

After application, the applicant proceeds to the simulated Huduma appointment system to verify details and book a service date and time.

3

Track queue in real time

The applicant dashboard automatically generates a queue ticket from verified records and supports remote tracking, arrival confirmation, and live queue updates.

4

Staff manage service delivery

Service officers monitor waiting applicants, call the next ticket, update service stages, and review operational reports and demand trends.

Project Scope

This platform is designed around the birth certificate service flow for Huduma Centre City Square. It includes simulated eCitizen and Huduma Kenya modules for academic demonstration and a centre-level queue tracking system for service coordination and transparency.
